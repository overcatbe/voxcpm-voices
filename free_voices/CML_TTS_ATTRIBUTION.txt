The recordings are audiobooks read by volunteers of the LibriVox project (public domain texts).

Cite as (from the dataset card):
  Oliveira, F. S., Casanova, E., Candido Junior, A., Soares, A. S., Galvao Filho, A. R. (2023).
  "CML-TTS: A Multilingual Dataset for Speech Synthesis in Low-Resource Languages."
  arXiv:2306.10097. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.10097

What is in this folder:
  cml_<lang>_<speaker>.wav   3 to 6 clean clips of ONE speaker joined with 0.3 s gaps (about 20-30 s,
                             24 kHz), used as a cloning reference. <speaker> is the dataset's speaker id.
  cml_<lang>_<speaker>.reference.txt   the transcripts of the clips that had one (some have none)

Speakers are anonymous LibriVox volunteers. The dataset gives no gender label; "lower/higher voice" was
guessed from median pitch only. Two files can never be the same person because each is a different speaker id.

Status: candidates, not approved. Listen before using any of them in a product, and check that the
model's own license allows the intended commercial use.
